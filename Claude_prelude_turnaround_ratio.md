# The Turnaround Ratio

*A prelude for the general reader*

---

There is a moment in the life of every thrown stone when upward becomes downward. The stone does not pause to deliberate. The arithmetic simply completes itself, and what was rising begins to fall. We do not call this mysterious. We call it gravity.

The framework asks you to consider that the universe is a thrown stone.

Not metaphorically. Mechanically. Everything that exists — every galaxy, every particle of dark matter, every quantum of energy, visible and invisible — was flung outward in a single eruption event from a pre-existing region of space, and is now moving through a cycle that has a turnaround, exactly as the stone does, for exactly the same reason.

The question worth asking is this: does gravity have enough grip to actually do this? Is the gravitational inventory of the universe sufficient to halt the expansion and call everything home?

We can calculate the answer. And the answer is unambiguous.

---

## The Turnaround Condition

At the moment of turnaround — when outward flight pauses before the long return — expansion velocity equals zero. This is a boundary condition with a precise mathematical home in the Friedmann equation, the foundational equation of cosmological dynamics:

$$H^2 = \frac{8\pi G}{3}\rho_{total} - \frac{kc^2}{a^2}$$

Setting $H = 0$ at the turnaround moment and solving for the density required to halt expansion:

$$\rho_{crit,turn} = \frac{3c^2}{8\pi G \, a_{crit}^2}$$

This is the gravitational ask: the mass-energy density that gravity must be able to muster in order to stop everything in its tracks.

---

## What We Already Know

From the bifurcation condition derived in Chapter 7 — from entirely different considerations, concerning the field configuration at which the eruption event fires — we already have a turnaround scale factor:

$$\frac{a_{crit}}{a_{now}} \approx 3.658$$

This number was not chosen to make the turnaround calculation work. It emerged from the restart threshold problem, from the shape of the potential well governing the phase transition. It is an independent result, derived for independent reasons.

Substituting into the turnaround condition:

$$\rho_{crit,turn} = \frac{\rho_{crit,now}}{(3.658)^2} \approx \frac{\rho_{crit,now}}{13.38}$$

---

## The Ratio

The total mass-energy inventory — visible matter, dark matter, dark energy, everything the universe contains — is present at turnaround. The gravitational grip required to halt expansion and initiate the return amounts to:

$$\text{Ratio} = \frac{1}{13.38} \approx 0.0747$$

**Roughly one part in thirteen of the total inventory.**

No new number appears here. The 13.38 is simply $3.658^2$ — our turnaround scale factor, squared. The square arises naturally because gravitational potential scales as $a^2$ in the Friedmann equation at the turnaround condition. One result, derived once, for a different purpose, doing double duty without being asked.

Gravity, at the scale of the full cycle, needs to engage only one part in thirteen of everything that exists in order to shepherd the entire universe home.

It has this. Comfortably. With inventory to spare.

---

## What This Means for Dark Energy

The standard cosmological model observes that the expansion of the universe is accelerating. To account for this observation it requires a repulsive force — dark energy — consuming approximately 68% of the total mass-energy inventory. The origin of this force is unknown. Its nature is uncharacterized. It is, in the most precise sense, a placeholder: a name given to an observation that the framework cannot otherwise explain.

The turnaround ratio suggests a different reading of the same observation.

An observer inside an expanding closed cycle, not knowing that the cycle has a turnaround, not knowing that they are approaching apex, would measure exactly what we measure: expansion that appears to be accelerating. From inside the ride, approaching the top of the arc, the deceleration that is coming registers first as continued acceleration. The instruments are accurate. The interpretation is mistaken.

Dark energy, on this reading, is not a constituent of the universe. It is a kinematic artifact — the signature of an observer who does not yet know which way is down.

The one part in thirteen is enough. It was always enough. Gravity has had this from the beginning.

## A Note on Method

This result was not engineered. The turnaround scale factor $a_{crit}/a_{now} \approx 3.658$ was derived in Chapter 7 from the bifurcation condition on the restart potential — a calculation concerned with when the eruption fires, not with whether gravity can halt the expansion. When that number was subsequently placed into the Friedmann turnaround condition, the ratio it produced was not adjusted, not fitted, not chosen. It simply appeared.

A framework that produces consistent results from independent derivations, without tuning, is behaving the way a framework should behave when it is tracking something real.
